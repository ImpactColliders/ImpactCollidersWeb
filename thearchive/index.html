About the Event

The Archive is an interactive community storytelling exhibition connecting seniors and youths through art, conversation, and technology. Seniors share personal memories through an AI-assisted storytelling platform, while youths create illustrated portraits inspired by their stories — preserving lived experiences as a collective archive and celebration of identity, memory, and legacy.

Our goal is to empower seniors to embrace technology and preserve their stories, promote intergenerational bonding between youths and seniors, encourage social interaction and storytelling, and support and showcase Singapore's arts scene.

The Archive welcomes seniors interested in learning and trying new experiences, youth volunteers, artists, and storytellers, and families looking to preserve memories across generations.
