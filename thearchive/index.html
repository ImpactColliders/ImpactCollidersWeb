About Event

The Archive is an interactive community storytelling exhibition that connects seniors and youths through art, conversation, and technology.

Seniors are invited to share personal memories and life experiences through an AI-assisted platform that transcribes, summarises, and categorises their stories — creating a collective archive of lived experiences.

Youths create illustrated portraits of the seniors, exhibited alongside their stories as a celebration of identity and memory, and given as a keepsake. Together we read and share one another's stories — fostering empathy, understanding, and stronger community bonds.
