Pay it Forward

Support seniors through the arts by donating to The Bloom Fund. Your donation funds one or more tickets to The Archive for senior beneficiaries, nominated by you or allocated by us.

Qualifying donations may be eligible for 250% tax deduction.

How It Works

Four simple steps to support a senior through The Archive. Each ticket costs $100.

1

Contact us, and we’ll provide the detail

2

Make a donation to The Bloom Fund

3

You may nominate a senior, or let us allocate the ticket

4

Eligible donors may receive 250% tax deduction

Sponsor Benefits

Organisations or individuals donating more than $1,000 may be eligible for sponsor benefits, including logo or name acknowledgement and promotional benefits.

We also welcome non-cash sponsorships.

For sponsor or in-kind support enquiries, please email us.
