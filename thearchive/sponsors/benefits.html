The Archive Sponsorship Prospectus The Archive 赞助方案

Where memory meets imagination. 记忆与想象交汇之处。

The Archive helps seniors preserve their life stories and leave a legacy through the arts and AI. The Archive 帮助长者通过艺术与 AI 保存自己的人生故事，并留下属于自己的传承。

In a guided workshop, participants take part in craft activities and meaningful conversations while youth artists draw their portraits. AI then helps capture and organise their stories into a shareable digital archive. 在引导式工作坊中，参与者将参与手作活动和有意义的交流，青年艺术家会为他们绘制肖像。AI 随后协助记录并整理他们的故事，形成可分享的数码档案。

Each participant takes home a hand-drawn portrait, their personal story, and their craft piece. Every sponsored ticket supports a senior beneficiary, promotes active ageing, and helps preserve Singapore’s heritage and life stories. 每位参与者都能带走一幅手绘肖像、自己的人生故事，以及亲手完成的手作作品。每一张赞助门票都支持一位长者受益人，促进积极乐龄，并帮助保存新加坡的文化传承与人生故事。
