The Archive Sponsorship Prospectus The Archive 赞助方案

Where memory meets imagination. 记忆与想象交汇之处。

500 tickets available 共 500 张门票

The Archive is an interactive community storytelling exhibition that brings seniors and youths together through art, AI-assisted oral history, and live portraiture. Seniors share their life stories in the languages most natural to them, while youth artists create portraits inspired by those memories. The Archive 是一场互动式社区故事展览，通过艺术、AI 辅助口述历史和现场人像创作，让长者与青年相遇。长者可以用自己最熟悉的语言分享人生故事，青年艺术家则从这些记忆中汲取灵感，创作专属人像。

Each sponsored ticket allows a senior to take part in the experience and receive a keepsake portrait, while contributing to a permanent digital archive of Singaporean life stories. 每一张赞助门票都能让一位长者参与体验，并获得一幅值得珍藏的人像作品，同时为新加坡生活故事的永久数码档案库留下珍贵记录。

Why Partner with The Archive 为何与 The Archive 合作

Four reasons your support makes a lasting difference. 您的支持将从四个方面带来长远影响。

Technology for Good 科技向善

Support a human-centred use of AI that helps seniors preserve their stories across languages and generations. 支持以人为本的 AI 应用，帮助长者跨越语言与世代，把自己的故事保存下来。

Digital Inclusion for Seniors 长者数码共融

Help seniors meaningfully engage with technology through a warm, accessible, and arts-led experience. 通过温暖、易参与且以艺术为引导的体验，帮助长者更有信心地接触科技。

Intergenerational Bonding 跨代连结

Bring seniors and youths together through storytelling, portrait-making, and shared conversation. 通过故事分享、人像创作和真诚交流，让长者与青年建立真实连结。

Singapore’s Art Scene 新加坡艺术生态

Support youth artists, community art, and the preservation of local memories as part of Singapore’s cultural heritage. 支持青年艺术家、社区艺术，以及作为新加坡文化遗产一部分的本地记忆保存。
