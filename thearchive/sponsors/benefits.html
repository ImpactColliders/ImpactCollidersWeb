The Archive Sponsorship Prospectus

Where memory meets imagination.

500 tickets available

The Archive is an interactive community storytelling exhibition that connects seniors and youths through art, AI-assisted oral history, and live portraiture. Seniors share their life stories in their own languages, while youth artists create portraits inspired by their stories.

Each sponsored ticket allows a senior to take part in the experience and receive a keepsake portrait, while contributing to a permanent digital archive of Singaporean lived experiences.

Why Partner with The Archive

Four reasons your support makes a lasting difference.

Technology for Good

Support a human-centred use of AI that helps seniors preserve their stories across languages and generations.

Digital Inclusion for Seniors

Help seniors meaningfully engage with technology through a warm, accessible, and arts-led experience.

Intergenerational Bonding

Bring seniors and youths together through storytelling, portrait-making, and shared conversation.

Singapore’s Art Scene

Support youth artists, community art, and the preservation of local memories as part of Singapore’s cultural heritage.
