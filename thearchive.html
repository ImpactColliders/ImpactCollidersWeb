What is The Archive?

The Archive is our space to slow down and trade notes — on the problems we're each chipping away at, the games and tools we're building, and the people behind them. Expect short talks, an open floor, and plenty of room to actually talk to someone new.

It's for founders, makers, students, and friends of Impact Colliders. No pitch decks required.
